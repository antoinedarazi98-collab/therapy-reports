Upload Existing Report

Upload an old report so the app can better understand this patient's needs, clinical requirements, previous findings, and therapy history.

Uploaded old reports are saved as patient memory only. They help the AI understand the patient, but they are not used for report statistics or progress charts. For statistics, the better method is to create a Custom Assessment inside the app and copy/paste the report data into your preferred structure.
