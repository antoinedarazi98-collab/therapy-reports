Security & Privacy

Your clinical data safety is a core priority

Updated June 3, 2026

Therapai is designed for sensitive clinical work. Patient files, reports, session notes, appointments, payments, and clinic profiles stay private, organized, and accessible only to the right people.

Protected patient information

Therapai is built around patient confidentiality. The platform protects data during login, while stored in the database, and while being accessed by authorized users.

AI privacy controls

When AI helps write reports or prepare sessions, the patient's real name is always removed or replaced before the request is sent to the AI. Therapists can keep clinical writing useful without sending the patient's identity.

Controlled access and roles

Clinic owners, therapists, and assistants can have different permissions. This makes it possible to control who can view patients, edit reports, manage payments, change profiles, or remove access.

Security built into everyday work

Therapai protects clinical data while therapists write reports, prepare sessions, manage patients, and use Evy. The protection is part of the workflow, not an extra step.

For the technically inclined

Therapai uses a layered security model for clinical data.

  • Encrypted data in transit using HTTPS.
  • Encrypted sensitive data at rest in the database.
  • Secure account sessions with protected authentication.
  • Role-based access controls for owners, therapists, and assistants.
  • Patient-name anonymization before AI requests.
  • Protected backups and controlled account deactivation.
  • Audit-ready records for important clinical and account actions.
Therapai is configured according to the healthcare privacy rules in the country where the clinic operates. The goal is to support clinical confidentiality while keeping the workflow simple for therapists.
